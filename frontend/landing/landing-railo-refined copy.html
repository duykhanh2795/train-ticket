Booking

Do I need to print my ticket?

No. Just show the QR code on your phone together with the ID you used when booking.

How long is my seat held?

Once you select a seat, it's held for 10 minutes. If you haven't paid by then, the seat is released to other passengers.

Are there separate fares for children and seniors?

Yes. When you add passengers, you enter their ages and the system applies the right fare under current regulations.

Can I book a round trip?

Yes. After choosing your outbound train, pick a return date and book both directions in one payment.

Payment

Which payment methods are supported?

Domestic ATM cards, Visa and Mastercard, e-wallets and bank transfer via QR code.

Will I receive an invoice?

Yes. Enter your invoice details at checkout and an electronic invoice is emailed to you within 24 hours.

Does the displayed price include service fees?

Yes. The price in the search results is the total you pay, with nothing added at the last step.

Changes, refunds and disruptions

How do I change or refund a ticket?

Open My tickets and choose the ticket you want to change or refund. The fee depends on how long before departure you do it, and it's shown clearly before you confirm.

What happens if my train is cancelled or delayed?

You get an email and text message as soon as anything changes. For a cancelled train you can switch to another one or get a full refund.

What if I miss my train?

Contact support as early as you can. Depending on your ticket type, you may be able to move to another train for a fee.
