Three stepsand you have your ticket.

Step 01

Find your train

Enter your departure station, destination and travel date. Available trains and fares show up instantly.

Step 02

Pick a seat and pay

Choose a seat or berth on the coach layout, then pay by card, e-wallet or QR bank transfer.

Step 03

Board with your QR code

Your ticket is emailed to you and saved in your account. Just scan the code when you board.
